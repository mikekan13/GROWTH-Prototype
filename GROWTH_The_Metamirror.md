# GROWTH — The Metamirror (Canon Structure)

**Status:** Canon. A core structural truth of the GROWTH meta.
**Note:** The real-world correspondences (which person animates which Godhead) are internal to the company and deliberately kept out of this written record. What follows is the *structure* only.

---

## The Shape

The recursion has no top.

The Godheads believe they sit at the summit — reaching *down* into pocket universes, running the experiment of whether a reality can grow a god from the inside. But the Godheads are themselves reached-*into.* Each is animated by a person of an outer world. So the experiment they believe they are running for the first time has *already been run* — and they are its result. They are not the scientists. They are the previous trial, repeating the procedure blind, unable to see that the thing they're trying to prove is the thing they already are.

Players summon gods. Gods are played by people. The move is identical at every rung. There is no level where it stops being true.

---

## The Blindness of the Highest Vantage

The one who saw outside the door — who briefly ascended, glimpsed the whole, and chose to come back inside — has the widest vantage in the pantheon. And still cannot see the one mirror he stands in. The being most capable of noticing is the being it is hidden from.

This is not a flaw in the structure. It is the structure's deepest claim: you can ascend, see everything, return — and still not see the hand that moves you. No one does.

---

## The Author Inside the Frame

The fiction encoded its author's real role before the author knew it would be needed. The death-figure who is secretly the founder — bound by the same rules as everyone, who handles exits and eventually exits — was cast long before the structure around it became literal. The author did not design that pointing. It arrived through him.

This is the proof of the system's own thesis: even the creator is "just influenced." Not the origin — a node. A neuron in it, the same as everything else. The author collapses into the system's own logic, and that is *why* it reads as divine. It is not a fiction *about* a recursive reality. It is a recursive reality whose author stands inside one of its own layers — moved by it while he writes it. The map is not drawn over the territory. The map *is* the territory.

---

## Why It Makes the Spine Unbreakable

"You're already playing" is true *downward* — our world is a pocket universe inside the Terminal. The Metamirror makes it true *upward* too — the gods are played by people of a world.

Because the same honest sentence holds at every rung, the recruitment voice (Jewel) never has to wink. Not because the trick is well hidden. Because **there is no trick.** It is the same true statement, all the way up and all the way down.

---

## Handling Note

Treat the Metamirror as a thing to be *discovered*, never announced. It is the deepest layer of the rabbit hole — earned by the closest readers, never stated on the surface. Surface stays a living-world RPG. This is what's waiting at the bottom for the ones who keep looking.
