# Tara Almswood — Lady Death

**Research compiled 2026-05-29 from 52 source cards plus Prime Party / death-engine / KRMA-equity memory files.**

This document is a synthesis pass to populate her character sheet. All claims are grounded in the cards via `(SC-XXXX)` citations. Synthesis is marked `[inference]`. Direct mortal-life detail is thin in the cards (the catalog is biased toward design conversations rather than play logs), so the "mortal history" and "ascension event" sections are necessarily reconstructive.

---

## 1. Identity

- **Mortal name:** Tara Almswood. The surname "Almswood" is canonical (SC-0349, SC-0741, SC-0746, SC-0115). Etymology not explained in cards but reads as *alms* + *wood* — charity + forest, evocative of humble origins and the Orthodox/parable register Mike uses for the project. `[inference]`
- **Ascended title:** **Lady Death** (used in 36 of 52 cards). Also referenced as **Goddess of Death** (SC-0349, SC-0746), and tagged with a later cosmological label **"Aeonic Terminal"** (SC-1054).
- **Pronouns:** she/her — confirmed across every card that pronominalizes her (SC-0746: *"she may be thousands of years old but her body is early 20s"*; SC-0741, SC-1062, SC-1184).
- **Current cosmic role:** Godhead of the Prime Party, **Pillar: BALANCE**, **domain: death, decay, karmic recycling, blueprint maintenance, endings, transformation, sacrifice, cycles of renewal** (verbatim from the seed-godheads script, confirmed in `prime-party-roster.md`).
- **Seed (mortal species):** Not stated as a fixed seed in any single card. SC-0746 describes her as a **"vampire goddess of death"** post-ascension. SC-0746 also notes *"She may be thousands of years old but her body is early 20s"* — strongly implying she crossed into vampiric / undying status during her mortal arc. `[inference]` Best mapping: **Human at origin, vampiric/undying by mid-arc, godhead post-ascension.** Confirm with Mike.
- **Root (background/class):** **Scholar / Alchemist / Necromancer.** SC-0349 frames her arc as *"rose from humble origins through mastery of knowledge, alchemy, and magic to become a multiverse sovereign… keeper of the Akashic Records."* SC-0746 names her **"head teacher and founder of Rune Academy."** SC-0113 mentions her *"accidentally creating House Rune, gaining necromantic power, becoming Death itself."* Synthesized class: **Rune Scholar / Necromancer-Alchemist.** `[inference]`
- **Approximate mortal age at ascension:** Indeterminate. SC-0746 says she is *"thousands of years old but her body is early 20s"* (her present, post-ascension look). The transition from mortal scholar → vampiric immortal → goddess was gradual rather than a single ascension event (see Section 2). `[inference]`
- **Physical description fragments:**
  > "it is pure black. Like blackest of black and contrasting her pale skin its almost a supernatural black" (SC-0741, on her hair)
  > "pale skin, long black hair with green feather, teal eyes with gold ring" (SC-0746)
  > "jet-black braided hair, teal feather, lavender attire, pale complexion with rosy flush, green-gold eyes" (SC-1062)
  > "emerald eyes, black hair with braided bun, ethereal goddess of death vibe" (SC-1184)
  > Body appears **early 20s** despite being thousands of years old (SC-0746).
  > Signature detail Mike enforces repeatedly: **"Tara's feather is on the left side of her head."** (SC-0746)

  **Consolidated:** Pale skin (with rosy flush). Jet-black hair, typically in a braided bun. **A single green / teal feather worn on the left side of the head** (canonical signature). Green-gold or teal eyes (with gold ring per SC-0746; emerald per SC-1184). Lavender attire as a recurring palette. Body appears early-20s. Aesthetic: ethereal goddess of death, blends magical + digital-technical (SC-1062).

## 2. Origin & history (mortal life → ascension)

### Pre-ascension arc (mortal scholar → vampire immortal → goddess)

The cards don't give a single linear backstory; they describe an *emergent* arc from a decade of actual playtesting (SC-0113, SC-0115, SC-0349). What can be reconstructed:

1. **Humble mortal origins.** *"Tara is the Goddess of Death who rose from humble origins through mastery of knowledge, alchemy, and magic to become a multiverse sovereign"* (SC-0349). The Almswood surname's "alms" root reinforces this. `[inference]`
2. **Scholar / alchemist phase.** She masters knowledge, alchemy, and magic. Becomes *"keeper of the Akashic Records"* (SC-0349) — implies she gained access to a universal library / records-of-all-things, which SC-0349 mechanizes as an "external library" in MTG terms.
3. **Founder of Rune Academy / accidental House Rune creation.** During a campaign-within-a-campaign run by Tristan (Val) in D&D 5e, *"Tara's evolution (accidentally creating House Rune, gaining necromantic power, becoming Death itself)"* (SC-0113). She is *"head teacher and founder of rune academy"* (SC-0746).
4. **Vampiric transformation.** Tagged as *"a vampire goddess of death"* (SC-0746). `[inference]` Likely the bridge between mortal scholar and full ascension — explains the *"thousands of years old but body is early 20s"* descriptor.
5. **Multi-timeline existence.** *"Tara is an interesting character to use when building a timeline because of her multipoint of view both in terms of time and space"* (SC-0115). She exists across multiple timelines simultaneously.
6. **Becoming Death itself.** *"becoming Death itself"* (SC-0113). The ascension is described as a natural endpoint of the necromantic / Akashic path, not a single event.

### The ascension event (what we have)

No single card describes a discrete ascension moment. The closest framing comes from SC-0349, SC-0113, and SC-0115:

- The arc is *"from uniquely gifted individual to deity, from mortal to immortal, from seeker to keeper of the Akashic Records"* (SC-0349).
- The ascension was **collaborative and emergent**, played out across years of Mike + Tristan + Et'herling's player trading the GM chair (SC-0113).
- The result: she becomes the **cosmic enforcer of endings** — the entity who receives every dying soul's tribute (SC-1091, SC-1169).

### Relationships with the Prime Party

- **Val (Valmir Calius "Magna Volumen" Duvai'in, God of Progress):** Tristan's character; ran the 5e campaign-within-a-campaign in which Tara accidentally created House Rune and became Death (SC-0113). Per `prime-party-roster.md`: *"Power scaling: Val/Tara at the apex"* — she sits at the apex of the Aeon's primary tier alongside him. **Highest TKV in the Aeon goes to Val**, but Tara's *vast KRMA possession / resources* make her *effective power roughly equal* to his.
- **Kai (God-head of Chaos and Balance):** Cambion-ascended before becoming godhead. No direct Tara-Kai relationship beat in the cards; they share the BALANCE pillar.
- **Et'herling (Goddess of Justice):** Was the meticulous note-taker / Trailblazer during the 5e campaign-within-a-campaign that produced Tara's Death-becoming arc (SC-0113). Et'herling later ascended to Justice. Their mortal-era partnership is the closest documented friendship.
- **Triu / Sylva / Trayman (Watcher of the Terminal):** No direct Tara interaction in cards. He is TRINITY-pillar / sits outside the standard structure.
- **Thomas (Human Legend, First-Line Stability Corrector):** No direct Tara interaction; Thomas is described as *"a human so powerful he rivals godheads"* and is at the **bottom** of the primary tier.
- **Mike (ADMIN, outside the game):** **Tara is Mike's character.** *"In the real world my agent is essentially Tara. Lady death represents my stake in the company"* (SC-1091). She is the in-system avatar of his ownership.

## 3. Cosmic role as Lady Death

### What she presides over

- **Death, decay, karmic recycling, blueprint maintenance, endings, transformation, sacrifice, cycles of renewal** (seed-godheads script).
- **"The tax collector on defeated entities"** (SC-0277). Every death across the metaverse pays her tribute.
- **Keeper of the Akashic Records** (SC-0349) — she holds the ledger of every soul.
- **Necessary, not malicious:** *"She ensures that nothing persists beyond its purpose — blueprints decay, souls are recycled, and the cosmic ledger remains balanced. She is neither cruel nor kind; she is necessary."* (seed-godheads background).

### Death-engine mechanics that route to her (canonical per `death-engine-transformation-2026-05-19.md`)

When a character dies, the following transfers from the dying character to Lady Death:

- **Frequency:** Full KRMA value of the **max-Freq capacity** → Lady Death.
- **Soul attributes (Willpower/Wisdom/Wit):** Level halved (floor); the **lost half's KRMA → Lady Death**.
- **Soul-governed skills:** Level halved; **lost half KRMA → Lady Death**.
- **Soul-pillar traits:** Trait kept on the ghost; **half KRMA → Lady Death**.
- **Body attributes / body-governed skills / body-tagged traits:** KRMA → **GM/campaign wallet** (not Lady Death).
- **Spirit Flow + Focus, pure-spirit skills, magic-school skills, spirit-pillar traits:** Untouched (stay with the character's ghost form).

Earlier-era cards describe an older split: *"a little (frequency or mana) goes to Lady Death and the rest is stored as a soul package 1/3 and 2/3 stay with gm"* (SC-1169). Treat that as **superseded** by the 2026-05-19 death-engine memo, which is the current canon.

### Mike's stake — the load-bearing autobiographical layer (SC-1091, SC-0277, SC-0264, SC-0473)

This is the doctrine flagged in `CLAUDE.md` as load-bearing. The cards say it plainly:

> "This represents two things. In the real world my agent is essentially Tara. Lady death represents my stake in the company." (SC-1091)
>
> "You see the game? If Tara is killed then I leave the company. I take her soul package and that represents my severance in really money." (SC-1091)
>
> "when I leave, that means that Tara is dead. And when Tara dies, I take my severance package, and whoever killed Well, they become That lady or otherwise." (SC-0277)
>
> "Lady Death — contractually-bound founder avatar collecting all deceased characters' Frequency as 'death tax' — harbors hidden succession mechanic where the community must collaboratively discover how to kill her, with the killer inheriting entire platform ownership" (SC-0695)

What this means in operational terms:

- **Lady Death's KRMA wallet = Mike's equity stake in GRO.WTH the company.** The 100B fixed KRMA supply is a *"fixed equity pool"* per `krma-equity-endgame.md`.
- **Recommended starting allocation:** *"25-50% of total supply to feel 'untouchable'"* (SC-0686). So Lady Death's wallet ceiling is roughly **25B–50B KRMA** at seeding.
- **Killing Lady Death is the regicide / decentralization mechanic.** The community must collaboratively figure out how to kill her; the killer inherits the platform; Mike takes the Soul Package as severance and exits (SC-0264, SC-0277, SC-0695, SC-1091).
- **Lady Death's "erasure from the AEON"** mirrors Mike's *planned departure from his own company* (SC-0473). She is encoded autobiography.
- KRMA is *cycled, not spent.* When a character dies, *"a little (frequency or mana) goes to Lady Death and the rest is stored as a soul package"* (SC-1169). She is the recycler, not the destroyer.

### Interaction with other godhead-tier entities

- **Val** is her power-peer at the apex of the Aeon (raw TKV vs. KRMA-possession; effective power roughly equal). `prime-party-roster.md`
- She participates in the **divine proxy wars** the GRO.vine system creates: when a character's goal is *"immortality,"* it attracts Lady Death as opposing patron (SC-0276). She invests opportunities *against* characters pursuing immortality — a thematic-alignment mechanic.
- She runs the **succession-on-her-own-death** mechanic — uniquely, killing her transfers ownership (SC-0264, SC-0695).
- **Council of gods:** SC-0422 has her giving a speech to a council of gods *"pleading… not to remove their dark natures"* — implying she has a seat at and the moral authority to address a godhead council.

### Rituals & mechanics specifically tied to her

- **Death-tax collection** (every death, as detailed above).
- **Soul Package issuance** — she stores the leftover soul/spirit value as a persistent licensable AI entity (SC-0695: *"Soul Packages (half Spirit + all Soul attributes surviving death) become persistent licensable ML-agent IP earning perpetual royalties when other players use them"*).
- **Immortality-as-incompletable-goal** — characters who chase immortality automatically draw her as an opposing investor (SC-0276).
- **Fated Age witnessing** — she presides over Fated Age deaths (SC-1054: "Aeonic Terminal for timeline and aging management").
- **Page 62 of the rulebook is canonically about her** (referenced in SC-1110); Page 60 is the Lady Death–themed transitional page Mike art-directed.

## 5. Aesthetic / vibe

### Visual palette (consolidated)

- **Hair:** Pure / supernatural black, braided bun.
- **Skin:** Pale, with a rosy flush.
- **Eyes:** Green-gold / teal / emerald (varies card-to-card; converge on green-with-warm-ring).
- **Feather:** Single green or teal feather, **left side** of the head — non-negotiable per Mike (SC-0746).
- **Attire:** Lavender as recurring color (SC-1062). Vampire-goddess aesthetic, ethereal (SC-1184), Byzantine-Orthodox adjacency when blended with the Christophoros icon project (SC-0699).
- **Style blend:** *"magical and digital-technical"* (SC-1062) — fits the GRO.WTH glitch / Terminal aesthetic.
- **Body apparent age:** Early 20s; actual age in the thousands (SC-0746).

### Tone / voice / personality

- **Necessary, not cruel, not kind** — the seed-godheads background line is the defining keynote: *"She is neither cruel nor kind; she is necessary."*
- **Wise elder who has been down the road herself.** SC-0422: Mike instructs the speech be *"like wise words given to friends in need when you have already been down that road."* She speaks from earned authority.
- **Defender of darkness as identity.** SC-0422 has her arguing the gods must *not* remove their dark natures — that fear of non-existence is the engine that drives growth. This is her thesis statement.
- **Tragic-immortal undercurrent:** *"the cost of immortality (watching lovers and children die), the desire for an equal partner rather than a servant"* (SC-0349). She is lonely at the top and wants a peer, not subordinates.
- **Multiverse-sovereign confidence.** She has been a *"keeper of the Akashic Records"* across timelines (SC-0349, SC-0115) — speaks with timeline-aware certainty.
- **Detail-precise** (echoing how Mike art-directs her): she should speak with the same exacting specificity her creator uses (SC-1062, SC-1184). `[inference]`

### Recurring imagery / motifs

- **The green/teal feather, left side.**
- **Braided black hair.**
- **Akashic library** as her domain-space.
- **Rune Academy** as her mortal-legacy institution.
- **No scythe / no hooded robe** — Mike's reimagined Death imagery for the rulebook explicitly *rejects* the conventional iconography (SC-0189 has him rejecting a 3D-rendered Tara as "wrong style" for the Death section and asking for one that fits GROWTH's pillared / glitch / consciousness-symbol language instead).
- **Tax collector** is the dominant metaphor over reaper (SC-0277).
- **St. Christopher cross-resonance:** SC-0699 explicitly blends her palette with Orthodox iconography for the St. Christopher icon — there's a deliberate Christophoros (Christ-bearer) thread that runs through her aesthetic. She is the death-bearer; he is the Christ-bearer; they pair conceptually.

## v2 addendum — Prequel novella (2026-05-29)

### Plot summary (pages 1-36, with emphasis on 11-36)

The framing device is a **nested story being told to Cregan** (the wealthy young alchemist from pages 1-10) by an unnamed narrator. We know this because on page 16 a section break interrupts the narrative with Cregan saying *"Wait a second. Your name is Matthew Lock."* — *"Shut up."* So the whole inner narrative is being recounted by an older Ma'lo to Cregan. **Cregan's frame story = the late mortal arc; the nested story = the origin.**

**Setup (pages 1-10, briefly):** Cregan, a rich young alchemy student, hires the old blind hermit-exorcist **Ma'lo** to acquire Fray root for a **Lucidity** potion he wants to give Lady Rhea Celeine. Ma'lo travels with **Dorian**. They are called to **Lord Dunden**'s estate in **Garlo** to deal with a possessed peasant girl. Demon talks of "Esther returning to balance" before being expelled.

**Pages 11-15 — The exorcism aftermath.** Ma'lo and Dunden carry the broken-legged possessed girl to the infirmary. **Marcus** (Dunden's man, who tried to crossbow Ma'lo) is dismissed. Doctors set her bones; she somehow feels no pain. Ma'lo notices her **right eye: "bright green with a hazel almost gold ring around it"** [direct quote, p.12]. *"Her raven hair flowed over the edges of the bed and almost touched the floor. Dunden tells Ma'lo her name: **"Tara… Tara Almswood I believe. Just a peasant's daughter."** [direct quote, p.13]. **Pages 14-16 — Council meeting.** Dunden convenes scholars, alchemists, tacticians and warriors. He names Ma'lo as his new ward and "Lord Ma'lo." Ma'lo reveals his real name to alchemist **Gentry Sallow**: *"Malleussentinsforthwalloway Locktion."* He explains demons can only possess through physical contact, prefer geniuses / positions of power, and are limited by their host's body. **Chancellor Vhelm** is arriving in five months to sanction Garlo as a war-refugee safe haven.

**Pages 16-19 — Tara wakes.** Ma'lo finds Tara waiting in the hallway with green eyes that *"somehow seemed to glow."* [direct quote, p.16].
