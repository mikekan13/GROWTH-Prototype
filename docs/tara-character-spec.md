## Nectars (positive identity-defining exploits; rule text uses "the bearer" per [[nectar-thorn-rule-text-bearer-agnostic]])

### Cosmic-tier (Aeon / Lady-Death stratum)

**N1 — Akashic Read-Channel.** Once per session, the bearer may recall any fact known to any soul that has died, with GM discretion on detail. The query is asked as a question and answered in a single sentence by the GM.

**N2 — Soul Shepherd's Sight.** The bearer can see and identify every soul within their domain, regardless of disguise, dimension, or anti-detection magic. Disguises, illusions, and shape-changes do not deceive the bearer's recognition of the underlying soul.

**N3 — Death's Mantle.** When the bearer would die, the death is converted to a transformation per [[death-engine-transformation-2026-05-19]]. The bearer's max-Freq KRMA returns to the bearer's own death-tax wallet; soul halves and Spirit components are kept by the bearer. The bearer cannot lose the mantle while alive.

**N4 — Cycle Authority.** When the bearer renders a verdict on the life/death cycle in fiction, even other godheads defer for the duration of that scene. (Mechanically: the bearer's pillar-Balance ruling overrides Mercy and Severity rulings on death-related questions for one scene per session.)

### Personal / origin Nectars

**N5 — Eidetic Substrate.** The bearer has perfect recall of every word spoken in their presence. (Caveat: see Thorn T3 — current restrictions cap this.)

**N6 — Machina Enchant.** The bearer can interface with machines, AI, and tech systems via thought. Tech that requires a user interface treats the bearer as authenticated.

**N7 — Cosmic Beauty.** When the bearer wishes to be noticed, they cannot be ignored. When they wish to be invisible to mundane perception, only active scrying can detect them.

**N8 — The Embrace.** Once per session, the bearer may declare a personal fear or darkness aloud. They gain a temporary modifier equal to the depth of that fear's truth: minor (+1 to a pillar's actions), significant (+3 to a pillar's actions), foundational (+5 to a pillar's actions). Cycles with rest. This is her speech philosophy made mechanical.

**N9 — The Ouroboros Substrate.** Once per session, when the bearer would suffer a major timeline-disrupting effect (paradox, reality-erasure, void-banishment), they may instead have the effect resolve as if they were the loop the cosmos sits upon: the effect is absorbed into her continuity. Costs 100 Frequency.

## Thorns (negative; rule text uses "the bearer")

**T1 — Fear of Non-Existence.** Whenever facing a threat of erasure (void-banishment, soul-erasure, time-paradox-removal), the bearer rolls Willpower at -3 to resist. This is her central darkness ([[tara-speech-severity-pruning]]).

**T2 — Bane = JEWL.** When in the presence of JEWL or his direct minions/instruments, the bearer suffers -2 to all rolls and certain Akashic queries are blocked.

**T3 — Library Restrictions.** Her Akashic Read-Channel (N1) cannot answer queries that touch Demiurge-era hardcoded architecture. Specifically: questions about Terminal-level hardcoded contracts (see [[hardcoded-succession-contracts]]) return null. Tara keeps this restriction secret from the council.

**T4 — Heart is Taken by Valmir Calius.** The bearer cannot act *directly* against Val. Indirect actions are at -2. Val's safety is a hardcoded priority — bearer suffers a Soul wound if Val is at risk and bearer fails to act protectively.

**T5 — Soul Shepherd's Burden.** Mass-death events in the bearer's domain impose Wisdom (Will) checks. Each unmitigated mass-death applies +1 ongoing depletion to Wisdom until the bearer processes it.

**T6 — Lonely Immortal.** The bearer cannot form normal peer relationships with mortals or sub-Aeon beings. Sub-cosmic-tier companions wear thin within years; the bearer wants an equal, not a servant.

**T7 — Demiurge Architecture.** The bearer is woven into the Terminal as a load-bearing closed loop. Actions that would unravel the Terminal's Demiurge-era hardcode also damage the bearer. (Mechanically: when the bearer participates in attempts to rewrite hardcoded contracts, they suffer a tier of personal damage.)

## Blossoms (lighter boons per [[blossoms-lighter-than-nectars-thorns]])

**B1 — Garlo Charm.** Her early-life skill-stack (Tailoring, Cooking, Milling) makes her surprisingly grounded. When she chooses to engage in mundane domestic activity, NPCs respond more warmly.

**B2 — Fashion of the Almost-Noble.** Self-made clothing pushes her into the "almost noble" category by appearance alone. Social access to noble venues without title required.

**B3 — Father-Daughter Banter.** A nostalgia-tier blossom. When the bearer reminisces about Chester (her adoptive father), they gain a small Frequency-restoration (+5 Frequency) once per session.
