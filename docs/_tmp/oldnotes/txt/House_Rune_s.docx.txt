Additional rules for the LAI
-To qualify for LAI a citizen must be seen by a cleric health professional twice a year. This is to ensure preventive healthcare and reduce the number of deaths per year. It is also a great quality of life increase for our cherished people.

-A citizen can choose to not be resurrected. If so, a stipend will instead be given to his or her family = to 500GP. This basically gives a huge chunk of money to their family. (Redistributes wealth to our citizens) Realistically, it would cost the government the same amount to resurrect the person and in the long run, would save money as that person would be removed completely from the system and would no longer cost additional funds to resurrect. The one additional caveat to this lump sum payment is that the person would also be required to donate their body to House Rune for scientific and national defense purposes. :)

-During times of war or national emergencies, House Rune reserves the right to suspend LAI rights.
